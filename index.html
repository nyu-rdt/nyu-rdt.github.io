Meet the team NASA Lunabotics · NYU Tandon

An ambitious robot.
A hands-on education.

We’re a team of NYU undergraduates working across mechanical, electrical, and software engineering to take a lunar mining robot from an idea to the competition floor.

At NASA’s annual Lunabotics competition, that means digging, hauling, and depositing simulated lunar regolith. Along the way, we advance autonomous excavation research and share robotics with communities across New York City.

Meet Luna.

Luna is our 2026 lunar mining robot, designed and built by students at NYU.
